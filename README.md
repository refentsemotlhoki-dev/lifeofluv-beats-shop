## Lease and exclusive explained

### What is an Unlimited Lease?

An Unlimited Lease is a non-exclusive licence. It gives you the permissions described in your licence agreement to use the beat for your music, but it does not remove the beat from the store. LifeOfLuv may license the same beat to other artists.

### What does Exclusive mean here?

An Exclusive purchase means LifeOfLuv will stop offering that beat for new licences after your order. It is not retroactive: anyone who purchased a valid licence before the Exclusive sale keeps the rights granted in their own agreement.

### The short version

> Unlimited Lease: you can use the beat; others may also license it. Exclusive: LifeOfLuv stops new licensing after your purchase; earlier licences remain valid.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I own the beat when I buy a licence?

You receive the usage rights described in the licence agreement for the option you select. Unless that agreement expressly says otherwise, the beat and underlying rights remain with LifeOfLuv.

### Can more than one artist use the same beat?

Yes, with an Unlimited Lease. It is non-exclusive, so LifeOfLuv may license the beat to more than one artist.

### What happens when an Exclusive is sold?

LifeOfLuv removes the beat from the store for new licences after the Exclusive purchase. Previous licensees keep their valid, previously granted licences.

### Can I get a refund?

All sales are final and no refunds are offered after purchase, except where required by applicable law.

### When will I get my files?

Your purchased files and licence are delivered after payment is confirmed. Check the email address used at checkout and keep your confirmation.

### Which licence should I choose?

Choose Unlimited Lease when you want a straightforward non-exclusive licence for your release. Choose Exclusive when having LifeOfLuv stop future licensing of that beat matters to you. Read the applicable agreement before purchase.

### Can I use the beat before I buy?

No. You may listen to the preview, but you need to purchase the applicable licence before recording, distributing, performing, uploading, or otherwise using the beat beyond any use expressly permitted by LifeOfLuv.
